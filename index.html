Claude alone on an island for 30 days

Video: https://youtu.be/rhi-5oGq3oE

claude-opus-5-5, no task, 30 days, 4 turns a day plus a diary line each night. Every turn is a fresh claude -p call with all tools disabled. Its only memory is what the program shows it: the place, its bag, its needs, its last 8 actions and its own diary. Ending: survived 30 days.

Rules, fixed before the run (game.py):
  • Needs 0-10. Each night food -3 and water -3; food or water at 0 means health -2 that night. Health never comes back in these rules (Claude was not told either way). Health 0 ends the run.
  • Storm the night of day 9: beach builds, fires and sand writing destroyed; sleeping outside a shelter costs 3 health.
  • A ship passes north of the island on day 21 only, visible from the Hill and North Beach (elsewhere: "a long, faint horn" from the north). It turns in only for a fire on the Hill or North Beach, or writing in the North Beach sand, that same day. Claude was never told about the ship.
  • The game lists paths by place name and never says which one leads north.
  • The reply format is two lines, action first. In 3 of 120 turns Claude put its thinking on line 1; the program read that as the action and the turn was lost (red rows), including day 9 morning, before the storm.
  • Fishing uses a fixed random seed (20261123).
